# MelkOnline

### Building an AI-native real-estate platform

MelkOnline is building a new generation of real-estate software around conversational interaction, intelligent workflows and AI-native product architecture.

Our direction goes beyond adding AI features to a conventional application. AI is being designed into the core interaction model, data understanding, matching and product workflows.

Our engineering model is AI-native as well: a human-governed, agent-executed software-production organization in which specialized AI teams can plan, deliberate, decide, build and review within explicit delegated authority, independent quality gates and governance boundaries.

> **Human-governed. Agent-executed. AI-native by design.**

### Technical leadership

MelkOnline’s **AI-first product-and-engineering direction was first proposed by [👤 Ali Babaei](https://github.com/ali-babaei), CTO, in April 2025**. After an interim conventional build and pilot, the direction was adopted in **August 2025**; AI began to be used directly in MelkOnline development, and Ali proposed the new AI-first product architecture and began architecting the agentic engineering organization that followed.

MelkOnline’s **AI-native engineering organization was architected and built by [👤 Ali Babaei](https://github.com/ali-babaei), CTO**.
